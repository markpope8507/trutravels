Thailand Bucket List

🌕

Full Moon Party

The world’s most legendary beach party on Koh Phangan. Neon paint, fire dancers, and thousands of travellers dancing barefoot on the sand until sunrise.

🏝️

Island Hopping

Explore Thailand’s 1,430 islands — from the iconic Phi Phi to hidden gems like Koh Lipe. Crystal water, white sand, and zero stress.

🛕

Temple Visits

From the golden spires of the Grand Palace to the ancient ruins of Ayutthaya. Thailand’s 40,000+ temples are jaw-droppingly beautiful.

👩‍🍳

Thai Cooking Class

Learn to make pad thai, green curry, and mango sticky rice from scratch. You’ll never order takeaway the same way again.

🛖

Floating Bungalows

Wake up on an emerald lake surrounded by limestone mountains in Khao Sok National Park. One of the most magical stays in the world.

🥊

Muay Thai Lesson

Get your hands wrapped and step into the ring for an intro to Thailand’s national sport. Whether you’re a natural or completely useless, it’s an absolute laugh.

Need to Know

Thailand FAQs

When is the best time to visit Thailand?

The best time to visit is between November and March when it’s cooler and drier. However, Thailand is amazing year-round — the ‘rainy season’ (June–October) usually means a quick downpour in the afternoon followed by sunshine.

Do I need a visa to visit Thailand?

Most nationalities get 30–60 days visa-free on arrival. You’ll need to register for a Digital Arrival Card online within 3 days of arrival. Check the latest requirements for your nationality before travelling.

Is Thailand safe for solo travellers?

Absolutely. Thailand is one of the safest countries in Southeast Asia for solo travellers. It’s well set up for tourism, locals are incredibly friendly, and roughly 65% of our TruTravellers come solo!

How much spending money do I need?

Thailand is great value. Budget around £15–25 per day for food, drinks, and extras on top of your trip cost. Street food meals cost £1–2, beers £1–3, and activities like diving are much cheaper than back home.

What vaccinations do I need?

No vaccinations are legally required, but we recommend being up to date on Hepatitis A, Typhoid, and Tetanus. Consult your GP or a travel clinic at least 6 weeks before departure.

Can I use ATMs in Thailand?

Yes, ATMs are everywhere — even on small islands. They accept international cards but charge a 220 THB (~£5) fee per withdrawal. We recommend withdrawing larger amounts less frequently.

Is Thailand LGBTQ+ friendly?

Thailand is one of the most LGBTQ+ friendly countries in Asia. Bangkok has a vibrant scene, and you’ll find acceptance across the country. Same-sex marriage was legalised in 2024.

What’s the food like if I have dietary requirements?

Thai food is incredibly diverse and accommodating. Vegetarian and vegan options are widely available. Just let us know when you book and we’ll make sure everything is sorted.
