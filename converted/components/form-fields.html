Form fields — the control kit

Every form control, with the class to write. .field is the site’s general field system — use it for any new form. The finished forms built on it each have their own component: contact-form, waitlist-form, newsletter-signup.

1 · Controls

.fieldtext input. Label 10px/.2em pink Montserrat · control 14px, 10px radius, white/5 on a 1px border, pink border on focus.
type="email" / "tel"same field, typed. Gets the right mobile keyboard and free browser validation — always type an input rather than leaving it text.
textareasame field. Resizes vertically by default; add style="resize:none" inside a modal where the box can’t grow.
selectsame field with the native arrow replaced by the house chevron. Options render on navy so the open list matches the page.
.field-rowtwo fields side by side from 640px up, stacked below it. Only for genuinely paired values — first/last name, expiry/CVC.
.form-checkconsent / opt-in row. Wrap the whole thing in the <label> so the text is part of the hit area.

2 · States — hint, error, success

.field__hint12px grey under a control. Say what you need before they get it wrong — a hint beats an error.

At least 8 characters.

.field.is-err + .field__errred border on the control, red message under it. Say what went wrong and how to fix it — never just “invalid”.

That email is missing everything after the @ — try alex@example.com.

.form-submitpink, full width, uppercase 12px. Add .form-submit--auto to shrink it to its content. The label says what happens — “Join The Waitlist”, not “Submit”.
.form-donereplaces the form once it’s sent — tick in a pink disc, title, one reassuring line. Add .form-done--boxed when it sits on the page rather than inside a modal.

3 · The page-specific families

Three pages predate .field and keep their own, deliberately different treatments. Don’t mix them, and don’t reach for them on a new page — they exist to match the page around them.

FamilyUsed byHow it differsWhy
.fieldWaitlist, enquiry, contact us, anything newPink 10px caps label, 14px controlThe default. Start here.
.co-fieldCheckoutGrey 12px label, 16px control, 8px radiusReads as a retail checkout, not a brand page — the bigger control is easier to fill on a phone mid-purchase.
.acct-fieldAccount → profileGrey 13px sentence-case label, 14px controlSettings, not a pitch — quieter labels sit better in a long list of rows.
.acct-fld in .acct-fsAccount → bookings, “good to go”Compact 12px control in a bordered fieldsetMany short fields inside a card inside a tab — the normal size wouldn’t fit.
.footer__formFooter mailing listPill input + pill button, no labelOne field on a dark band; the placeholder carries it.
