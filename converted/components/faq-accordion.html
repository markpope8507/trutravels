FAQs

Where does the trip start & finish?

The trip begins in Bangkok and finishes in Phuket.

Which airport do I need to fly into?

You should arrive at Suvarnabhumi (BKK) which is the main international airport, or alternatively you can fly into Don Muang (DMK).

Are airport transfers included in the price?

Airport pickup on arrival day is included. If you need additional transfers, these can be arranged through your account or by contacting the sales team at info@trutravels.com up to 30 days before departure.

What is the closest airport to the end destination?

The closest airport is Phuket International Airport (HKT).

How do I return to Bangkok and what's the cost?

Return transportation isn't included. A ferry and bus combination costs around $40 USD (approximately 12 hours). Flying from Phuket costs roughly $80 USD but takes much less time.

What accommodation is provided?

The accommodation for this trip is all twin share private rooms across a mix of hotels, floating bungalows, and beach bungalows.

What currency is used?

The currency in Thailand is Thai Baht (THB). ATMs are widely available and most places accept card payments in tourist areas.

Is my money protected?

Yes. TruTravels is both ABTA and ATOL registered, ensuring your money is fully financially protected.

Do you have age limits?

The maximum age for this trip is 45. Our Backpacker trips are exclusively for ages 18–29. Classic trips welcome anyone 18–45.

Can you accommodate dietary requirements?

Absolutely. Just let us know about any dietary requirements when you book and we'll make sure everything is sorted.

Do many travellers go solo?

Roughly 65% of our TruTravellers come solo! Our trips are specifically designed with solo travellers in mind — you'll make lifelong friends from day one.

Are there any visa requirements?

Visitors must register for a Digital Arrival Card online within 3 days of arrival. Check the latest visa requirements for your nationality before travelling.
