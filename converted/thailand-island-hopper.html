Itinerary

1

Welcome to Thailand!

A city with as much history as it has traffic. Tonight you'll meet your group and tour leader on the famous Khao San Road — grab some local street food, sink a few cold ones, and get to know the people you're about to have the time of your life with.

  • Bangkok Hotel
2

Bangkok Temples & River Cruise

Start with a traditional boat journey through Bangkok's back canals and along the Chao Phraya River. Visit the stunning Wat Arun temple glowing in the morning light, then Wat Pho and its famous reclining Buddha. Tonight it's your call — rooftop cocktails or Bangkok's legendary nightlife.

  • Bangkok Hotel
  • 1x Breakfast
3

Massage & Overnight Train

Kick off with a traditional Thai massage before boarding the overnight train heading south towards Khao Sok National Park. There's something magical about falling asleep to the rhythm of the rails, watching Thailand's countryside slip by.

  • Overnight train to Khao Sok
  • 1x Breakfast
4

Khao Sok National Park

Wake up to one of Thailand's best-kept secrets — emerald lakes, towering limestone mountains, and floating bungalows straight out of a movie. Spend the day kayaking, swimming in impossibly green water, and soaking it all in. Dinner is a Thai buffet at the floating restaurant.

  • Khao Sok Floating Bungalows
  • 1x Breakfast, 1x Lunch, 1x Dinner
5

Bottle Beach Experience

One of our absolute favourite spots in the world — Bottle Beach, only accessible by boat, and today it's all yours. Swim, play volleyball, trek the jungle, or do absolutely nothing. As the sun goes down, a TruExclusive evening: dinner on the sand, cocktails, and a private fire show.

  • Bottle Beach Bungalows
  • Ferry to Koh Phangan + longtail to Bottle Beach
  • 1x Breakfast, 1x Dinner
6

Morning Chill & Muay Thai

No alarms today. Sleep in, soak up the beach vibes, or hike to the Bottle Beach viewpoint for jaw-dropping coastal views. In the afternoon, wrap your hands for an intro Muay Thai lesson — Thailand's national sport. Whether you're a natural or completely useless, it's a laugh.

  • Bottle Beach Bungalows
  • 1x Breakfast
7

Koh Phangan Island Boat Trip

Jump on a boat and spend the day island-hopping around Koh Phangan's stunning coastline. Stop at secret snorkelling spots, swim in crystal-clear coves, and soak up the sun on deck. Lunch on the boat. Back to shore just in time for sunset, then out for dinner and drinks.

  • Koh Phangan Beach Hotel
  • 1x Lunch
8

Koh Tao Bound

Speed boat across to Koh Tao — the diving capital of Thailand. Check into your spot near Sairee Beach, lined with cool little restaurants and beach bars. The afternoon is yours — rent a scooter, find a quiet beach, or just wander and take it all in.

  • Koh Tao Beach Resort
  • Speed boat to Koh Tao
9

Koh Nang Yuan Day Trip

Today's a big one. Boat trip to the iconic Koh Nang Yuan — three tiny islands connected by a white sandbar you've definitely seen on Instagram. Snorkel in some of the clearest water you'll ever see, or just chill on the boat. Group dinner tonight followed by beach-bar hopping.

  • Koh Tao Beach Resort
  • 1x Dinner
10

Get Active or Relax

Your last full day on Koh Tao — make it count. Head to Tanote Bay for some of the island's best swimming, try wakeboarding or cliff jumping, or claim a sun lounger and do absolutely nothing. No judgement either way.

  • Koh Tao Beach Resort
11

Travel Day to Phi Phi Islands

Say goodbye to Koh Tao and hop on the afternoon speed boat to the Phi Phi Islands. Check in, freshen up, and get ready for a group dinner on one of the most beautiful islands on the planet. The views here are absolutely unreal.

  • Phi Phi Island Hotel
  • Speed boat to Phi Phi
  • 1x Dinner
12

Maya Bay — The Beach

Full-day island excursion to Maya Bay — the filming location from The Beach with Leonardo DiCaprio. Snorkel at Monkey Beach, explore Viking Cave, and cruise past towering limestone cliffs. Lunch on the boat, sunset from the deck. One of those days you'll never forget.

  • Phi Phi Island Hotel
  • 1x Lunch
13

Phuket

Optional early morning viewpoint hike in Phi Phi — worth the effort if you're up for it. Then transfer to Phuket for your final afternoon of freedom. Relax by the pool, hit the beach, or explore town. Tonight is the big farewell — group dinner and a proper send-off in Patong.

  • Phuket Hotel
  • Ferry to Phuket
  • 1x Dinner
14

Chilled Check Out

And just like that, it's over — but not really, because you've just made friends for life, filled your camera roll with bangers, and had the kind of adventure most people only dream about. We'll help you sort onward travel, swap details with the crew, and say those bittersweet goodbyes.

Where You’ll Stay

Bangkok Hotel

Start your trip in a comfortable hotel in the heart of Bangkok, walking distance from Khao San Road and the city's best street food. Twin-share rooms with AC and hot showers.

Video

Khao Sok Floating Bungalows

One of the most magical stays on the trip. Wake up on an emerald lake surrounded by towering limestone mountains. Basic but beautiful — hammocks, wooden decks, and water all around you.

Bottle Beach Bungalows

Rustic beach bungalows right on the sand at one of the most secluded beaches in Thailand. Fall asleep to the waves, wake up to stunning views. Only accessible by boat.

Koh Tao Beach Resort

Your home base on the diving capital of Thailand. Simple, clean rooms steps from Sairee Beach — the island's best strip of sand, restaurants, and beach bars.

FAQs

Is this trip good for solo travellers?

Absolutely — the majority of our travellers join solo. By the first night on Khao San Road you'll have a whole crew of new mates.

What's the group size?

Groups are typically 12–18 travellers, all aged 18–35, led by a local Local Legend who knows the region inside out.

How much spending money will I need?

Budget roughly £25–40 per day for meals not included, drinks and optional extras. Thailand is very affordable.

Do I need a visa?

Most nationalities get 30 days visa-free on arrival, which comfortably covers this 14-day trip. Always check your own requirements before you travel.
